    <h2 id="sigiriya">Sigiriya</h2>
    <p>
      The climb up Sigiriya Lion Rock (UNESCO) is Sri Lanka's most famous experience — an
      ancient rock fortress with panoramic views over the jungle canopy. It anchors the
      Cultural Triangle and pairs naturally with Dambulla on the same day. Sigiriya features
      on day 1 of my <a href="{{ '/itinerary/' | relative_url }}#3-day-cultural">3-Day Cultural Triangle</a>
      itinerary and every longer tour.
    </p>

    <h2 id="dambulla">Dambulla</h2>
    <p>
      The Dambulla Cave Temple (UNESCO) shelters 153 Buddha statues beneath
      2,100-year-old painted murals — one of the most atmospheric sacred sites on the
      island, and an easy stop between Colombo and Sigiriya on the
      <a href="{{ '/itinerary/' | relative_url }}#5-day-highlights">5-Day Highlights</a> route.
    </p>

    <h2 id="polonnaruwa">Polonnaruwa</h2>
    <p>
      Sri Lanka's medieval capital (UNESCO) — the Gal Vihara rock temple and royal palace
      ruins are best explored in the morning before the heat. In season, an afternoon
      elephant-gathering safari at nearby Minneriya or Kaudulla makes a perfect pairing.
    </p>

    <h2 id="kandy">Kandy</h2>
    <p>
      The cultural capital: the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, the lakeside walk,
      Peradeniya Botanical Gardens, and evening cultural dance shows. I especially love
      taking guests through the spice gardens and markets around Kandy and Matale —
      <a href="{{ '/about/' | relative_url }}">food and spices are my passion</a>.
    </p>

    <h2 id="ella">Ella &amp; the Tea Country</h2>
    <p>
      Misty tea estates, the Nine Arch Bridge, the Little Adam's Peak hike, and Ravana
      Waterfall. This is also where Sri Lanka's famous scenic train rides run — I can drop
      you at the station, drive your luggage ahead, and meet you at the other end
      (<a href="{{ '/faq/' | relative_url }}">see the FAQ</a>).
    </p>

    <h2 id="galle">Galle &amp; the South Coast</h2>
    <p>
      The Dutch Fort at Galle (UNESCO) — ramparts, lighthouse, and colonial streets —
      plus the beaches of Unawatuna and Mirissa. The classic finish to the
      <a href="{{ '/itinerary/' | relative_url }}#5-day-highlights">5-Day Highlights</a> tour
      before returning to Colombo.
    </p>

    <h2 id="yala">Yala National Park</h2>
    <p>
      Sri Lanka's premier safari destination — leopards, elephants, and crocodiles.
      I arrange the jeep safari and coordinate the booking for you, and can either join
      you in the jeep or meet you after the park
      (<a href="{{ '/faq/' | relative_url }}">details in the FAQ</a>). Yala is the wild
      highlight of the <a href="{{ '/itinerary/' | relative_url }}#7-day-explorer">7-Day Island Explorer</a>.
    </p>

    <h2 id="beyond">Beyond the Highlights</h2>
    <p>
      Negombo's fish market and Dutch canal, Nuwara Eliya's "Little England" tea estates,
      Horton Plains and World's End, Trincomalee and the east coast beaches — if it's on
      the island, we can build it into a
      <a href="{{ '/services/' | relative_url }}#custom-tours">custom tour</a>.
    </p>
  </div>
